Rainbow Patrol v0.1.0-test.1
Playtest build — 2026-11-10
Git tag: v0.1.0-test.1

Three missions: Coast, Winter Valley and Storm City.
Selected H2 frost glass and Y1 concealed amber instrument illumination.
Original Sopwith foundation, painted scenery, AI wingman and WATCH DEMO.
Trainer defaults to off. In flight: 1–3 jumps to a mission; E clears the mission.
Either shortcut automatically enables Trainer and makes the entire run unranked,
even if Trainer is turned off again. Settings can also toggle Trainer.
Sound defaults to off. Scores are stored in the browser; trainer runs are unranked.
Version/date are visible on the title screen and Info page.
